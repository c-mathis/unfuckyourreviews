## Lead Organization

Each lead will have:
- `city` field (Sacramento, Roseville, Elk Grove, etc.)
- `zip` field (95816, 95678, etc.)
- `rating` field
- `reviewCount` field
- `reviewIssue` field (auto-generated by filter script)

You can segment campaigns by:
- City (e.g., send to Sacramento first, then expand)
- Zip code (target specific neighborhoods)
- Rating severity (< 3.5 stars vs 3.5-3.9 stars)
