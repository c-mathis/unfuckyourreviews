# Unfuck Your Reviews - Meta Ads Campaign

**Goal:** Generate qualified leads at $50-100 CPL targeting local business owners

**Pricing:** $399-500/mo (disclosed after form submission, in follow-up)

---

## Campaign Strategy

### Why Meta Ads for This Offer?

**Pros:**
- Precise audience targeting (business owners, specific industries)
- Visual storytelling (before/after review screenshots)
- Retargeting capabilities
- Lower CPC than Google Ads for awareness

**Cons:**
- Higher CPL than cold email (expect $50-150/lead vs $5-20 for email)
- Requires creative production
- Need larger budget to optimize ($1,500-3,000/mo minimum)

**Best use:** Complement cold email, scale once you validate offer-market fit

## Learning Phase Strategy

Meta's algorithm needs data to optimize. Here's how to get through learning phase fast:

### Week 1-2: Broad Testing
- Run 3-4 ad sets with different audiences
- Run 2-3 ad creatives per ad set
- Let algorithm distribute budget
- Goal: 50+ conversions per ad set to exit learning phase

### Week 3-4: Optimization
- Kill worst-performing ad sets (CPL > $150)
- Kill worst-performing ads (CPL > $125)
- Scale budget on winners by 20% every 3 days
- Add new creative variations to winning ad sets

### Week 5+: Scaling
- Consolidate budget into 1-2 best ad sets
- Test new creative every week
- Refresh creative every 2-3 weeks (combat fatigue)
- Expand to new audiences (lookalike, interest stacks)

## Meta-Specific Optimizations

### Mobile-First Everything

- 80%+ of traffic will be mobile
- Test ads on mobile before desktop
- Landing page must load in under 2 seconds on 4G
- Form must be thumb-friendly (large tap targets)

### Video Best Practices

- Hook in first 3 seconds (stat or pain point)
- Always use captions (80% watch muted)
- Keep under 60 seconds (ideal: 30-45 sec)
- Square format (1:1) works best in feed
- Vertical format (9:16) for Stories

### Ad Fatigue Management

- Refresh creative every 2-3 weeks
- Monitor frequency (keep under 3x/week)
- Rotate 3-5 active ads at all times
- Pause ads when CPL increases 50%+

## Integration with Cold Email

**Don't choose one or the other - do both:**

**Cold Email:**
- Higher intent (you found their review problem)
- Lower CPL ($5-20/lead)
- More personalized
- Harder to scale (caps at 500-1,000 emails/day)

**Meta Ads:**
- Lower intent (they found you)
- Higher CPL ($50-100/lead)
- Less personalized
- Easier to scale (just increase budget)

**Combine for maximum effect:**
1. Run Meta ads for brand awareness
2. Retarget Meta ad viewers with cold email (if you capture them)
3. Use Meta ads to build lookalike audiences from cold email customers
4. Run Meta ads to warm markets while cold email handles cold outbound

**Expected blended results:**
- 10-15 leads/week from cold email
- 5-10 leads/week from Meta ads
- 15-25 total leads/week
- 3-5 new clients/month
- $1,200-2,500 MRR added/month
