## Filtering Effect (Feature, Not Bug)

The "fucked" subject line will:

✅ **Pre-qualify leads** - Only business owners who appreciate directness reply
✅ **Stand out** - 99% of cold emails are boring, this isn't
✅ **Match brand** - Unfuck Your Reviews is the brand, not "Enhance Your Reputation Management"
✅ **Higher close rate** - People who reply are already aligned with your tone

❌ **Offend some people** - They'll unsubscribe (good, they weren't buyers anyway)
❌ **Slightly higher spam complaints** - But still <0.5% if list is clean

---

## Spam Risk Mitigation

**To keep deliverability high with aggressive subject:**

1. **Clean list** - Only verified emails (MillionVerifier does this)
2. **Good sending reputation** - Your domains are warmed (✅)
3. **High engagement** - Opens/replies signal to Gmail you're legit
4. **Low complaints** - Unsubscribe link in footer (Instantly adds auto)
5. **Gradual scale** - Start 30/day per inbox, scale weekly

**Expected metrics:**
- Spam complaint rate: 0.2-0.4% (target: <0.5%)
- Unsubscribe rate: 1-2% (higher than generic, but expected)
- Bounce rate: <5% (MillionVerifier keeps this low)
