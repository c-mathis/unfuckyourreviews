# A/B Test: Two Email Body Approaches

**Test Setup:** Split 10 inboxes into two groups (5 each)

**Test Duration:** 2 weeks

**Winner:** Highest positive reply rate

---

## VERSION A: Problem + Consequence (Original)

**Strategy:** Tell them what's wrong and why it matters

**Use on:** 5 inboxes

---

### Email 1 - The Callout

**Subject Lines (rotate all 4):**
```
Are your reviews fucked?
Are your reviews f*cked?
{{businessName}} - are your reviews fucked?
{{firstName}}, your reviews are f*cked
```

**Body:**
```
{{firstName}},

{{rating}} stars on Google? Most searches won't even show you.

Want to talk about it?

— Cameron
Unfuck Your Reviews
unfuckyourreviews.com
```

**Delay:** 3 days

---

### Email 2 - The Math

**Subject:** `Re: {{businessName}}'s reviews`

**Body:**
```
{{firstName}},

Quick math:

{{rating}} stars = 47% of people won't call
50 searches/month = 24 lost customers
24 customers × 30% close rate × $1,500 job = $10,800/month

We get you to 4.5+ stars in 90 days.

Want to see how?

— Cameron
```

**Delay:** 4 days

---

### Email 3 - Social Proof

**Subject:** `Last one`

**Body:**
```
{{firstName}},

What we did for a {{industry}} business in {{city}}:

3.4 stars → 4.6 stars in 90 days
Result: 120% more calls

Your competitors with 200+ reviews aren't lucky. They have a system.

Want one?

— Cameron
```

**Delay:** 4 days

---

### Email 4 - The Breakup

**Subject:** `Moving on`

**Body:**
```

Last email.

If you want help with {{businessName}}'s reviews, hit reply.

If not, do these three things:
1. Respond to every review
2. Text your last 10 customers
3. Address that negative review publicly

— Cameron
```

## How to Set Up A/B Test in Instantly

### Option 1: Two Separate Campaigns (Recommended)

**Campaign A:** "Unfuck Reviews - Problem/Consequence"
- Use 5 inboxes
- Version A emails
- 150 emails/day total (30 per inbox)

**Campaign B:** "Unfuck Reviews - Open-Ended"
- Use 5 inboxes
- Version B emails
- 150 emails/day total (30 per inbox)

**Benefits:**
- Clean data separation
- Easy to compare metrics
- Can pause one if performing badly

---

### Option 2: Single Campaign with Variants (Advanced)

Single campaign with both body variants:
- Instantly rotates between them
- Same lead list for both
- 50/50 split automatically

**Benefits:**
- One campaign to manage
- Auto-optimization

**Drawbacks:**
- Harder to track which version sent to which lead

## How to Decide the Winner

**After 2 weeks, calculate:**

**Winner = Highest "Net Positive Replies per 1,000 Emails"**

**Formula:**
```
(Positive Replies / Emails Sent) × 1,000 = Net Positive per 1K
```

**Example:**
- Version A: 120 positive replies / 1,500 emails = 80 per 1K
- Version B: 105 positive replies / 1,500 emails = 70 per 1K
- **Winner: Version A**

**But also check:**
- Close rate (booked calls / positive replies)
- Deal size (do Version B leads pay more?)
- Time to close (faster = better)

**If Version B has 10% fewer replies but 20% higher close rate → Version B wins**
