## Why This Works

**Email 1 is 3 lines:**
1. Problem (low rating)
2. Consequence (won't show in searches)
3. Question (want to talk?)

**Benefits:**
- Takes 10 seconds to read
- Impossible to misunderstand
- Low commitment ask ("talk about it" vs "buy this")
- Matches aggressive subject line tone
- Mobile-friendly (most people read on phone)

**Expected results:**
- Open rate: 55-65% (aggressive subject)
- Reply rate: 8-12% (simple = higher response)
- Positive reply rate: 60-70% (pre-qualified by subject)

**Net positive replies:** 30-40 per 1,000 emails (vs 20-25 with longer version)

---

## Email Flow Strategy

**Email 1:** Problem + Simple question (start conversation)
**Email 2:** Add urgency with math (show $$ impact)
**Email 3:** Add proof (they're not alone)
**Email 4:** Last chance (breakup)

Each email builds on the last, but Email 1 does 80% of the work.
